## [1.0.0] - 2026-11-23

The ffmpeg collection: everything from `ffmpeg-commands` merged in with its history, the old `notes/` turned into commented, tested snippets, and eleven theme folders, all tested on ffmpeg 9.0.2.

### Changed
- Relicensed from GPL-3.0 to MIT; earlier releases keep GPL-3.0 (#7)
- Test-harness markers moved out of the snippet headers into a manifest kept outside the repo; the `# Tested:` lines stay (#5)

### Added
- `ffmpeg-commands` merged in with its full history (44 commits), and the 29 loose scripts replaced by snippets in theme folders: conversion, resize-crop, overlay, stills-and-gifs, timing, effects, audio-visualization, image-sequence, batch, related-tools (#1)
- 18 commented, tested snippets converted from `notes/`, including a new `diagnostics/` folder; commands that repeated existing snippets became variants of them, and `notes/` is gone (#3)

### Fixed
- Errors in the original notes commands: a missing space in the ProRes 4444 profile flag, `aphasemeter`'s unlabeled outputs, typos in the 100-input mosaic (now generated by a loop with `xstack grid=`), `-level 3.1` for a 1080p baseline encode, and a glob that didn't batch (#3)
